About Palash Ranjan Roy

I am Palash Ranjan Roy, though many colleagues know me as Palash Roy in academic publications. I am currently pursuing my PhD in Computer Science at the University of Saskatchewan, Canada, where I also completed my MSc degree. My research interests lie at the intersection of software engineering and artificial intelligence, with a focus on code clone detection, refactoring methodologies, and applications of large language models in software development.

Originally from Bangladesh, I completed my BSc in Computer Science at BRAC University before moving to Canada to pursue graduate studies. Throughout my academic journey, I've been fortunate to receive several accolades, including the GSA Research Excellence Award, Best Thesis Award (MSc), and the People's Choice Best Paper Award at IWSC-2023.

I am affiliated with both the Software Research Lab (SRLab) and Interactive Software Engineering Lab (ISELab) at the University of Saskatchewan, where I collaborate with fellow researchers on innovative solutions to complex software engineering challenges.

Research Interests

  • Code Clone Detection: Developing novel techniques to identify semantic and cross-language code clones
  • Large Language Models: Exploring applications of LLMs in software engineering tasks
  • Software Refactoring: Creating tools and methodologies to improve code quality
  • AI for Software Engineering: Leveraging artificial intelligence to enhance developer productivity
  • Software Analytics: Analyzing codebases to derive insights for better software development
